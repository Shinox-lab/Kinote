# Editing together

<ClipVideo name="co-edit" label="Two people edit one page at the same time" />

Any number of people can work on the same page at the same time, and so can AI agents. Nobody has to wait for anybody
else, and nobody's work is overwritten.

## How it works

1. Open a page. Anyone else on it shows at the top of the page.
2. Start typing. Other people's cursors appear in the text with their names, each in their own colour, and their
   words appear as they type them.
3. Kinote saves everything as you go. There's no Save button for pages.

## Seeing who wrote what

Open the page status chip under the title and choose **History** to see every saved version of the page, who made it
and when. **Changes** shows what's different since the page was last indexed.

## Mentions

Type `@` and a name to mention someone. Once the page is saved, they get a notification that takes them to it.
