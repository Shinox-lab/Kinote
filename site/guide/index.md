# Workspaces, spaces and pages

Kinote keeps your team's knowledge in one place: a **workspace**. Inside it, pages are grouped into **spaces**, and
pages can sit under other pages.

## Workspaces

A workspace belongs to one team or company. Everything in it (pages, files, comments, members) stays inside it, and
people from other workspaces can't see any of it. You can belong to more than one workspace and switch between them
from the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar.

## Spaces

A space is a shelf for related pages, such as Engineering, Clients or Handbook. Each space decides:

- **who can see and edit it**, through the people and groups it's shared with (see [Members and roles](./members));
- **whether changes need review** before they count (see [Review and page status](./review)).

To make a space, click **+** next to **Spaces** in the sidebar. To rename or archive one, right-click it.

Every workspace also has an **Inbox**. Files you capture or upload without choosing a space land there. Only you and
the workspace admins can see what you put in your Inbox.

## Pages

A page is a document you write in, like a page in a notebook. To add one:

1. Hover a space or page in the sidebar and click the **+** button.
2. Choose **New page**.
3. Type a title and start writing.

Type `/` on an empty line to see what you can add: headings, lists, tables, callouts, a drawing, a view of a
collection, a link to another page and more.

To give a page more room, open the **…** menu at the top right and pick a **Width**: **Default**, **Wide** or
**Full**. The width belongs to the page, so everyone who opens it sees the same layout.

Kinote saves as you type. A little while after you stop, the page is **indexed**, which means search and Ask can find
what you wrote. The chip under the title tells you where the page stands. [Review and page status](./review) explains
each one.

## The sidebar

| Item | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your starting point in the workspace |
| **Capture** | Write something once and let Kinote file it ([Capture and routing](./capture)) |
| **Search** | Find pages and files, or get an answer ([Search and Ask](./search-and-ask)) |
| **Reviews** | Changes waiting for a reviewer, with a count |
| **Notifications** | Replies, mentions and other news, with a count of unread ones |
| **Settings & members** | People, groups and spaces (for admins) |
| **Spaces** | Your spaces and the pages inside them |
