# Capture and routing

<ClipVideo name="capture" label="A note is filed into two pages in two spaces" />

Capture is the fastest way to keep your wiki up to date. You write something down once (a decision, a call summary,
pasted meeting notes or a file) and Kinote's **routing engine** works out which pages it affects and edits them. You get
a receipt of everything it changed, with a reason for each change and an Undo button.

## Send a capture

1. Click **Capture** in the sidebar.
2. In **What do you want to file?**, type or paste your text. You can also click **Attach a file** to send a PDF, an
   Office document, a spreadsheet, an image or a text file, with or without text.
3. Optionally, choose:
   - **File as**: **Auto** lets Kinote decide, **New page** files it as a page of its own, and **Updates only** only
     edits existing pages.
   - **Limit to**: **Anywhere**, or one space. If you open Capture from a page, you can limit it to that page.
4. Click **Send**.

A capture can be up to 20,000 characters long.

## What happens next

Your capture appears below the box with its status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Waiting to route** | It's in the queue. |
| **Reading the file…** | Kinote is reading the text out of your file first. |
| **Routing…** | The routing engine is deciding where each fact belongs. |
| **Needs your answer** | The engine has a question for you (see below). |
| **Done** | Finished. The receipt shows what changed. |
| **Routing failed** | Something went wrong. The card says what. |

## Follow-up questions

When the engine isn't sure (for example, two pages could be the one you mean), it asks instead of guessing. The
question appears on the capture with a button for each option. Click the right one and routing carries on.

## The receipt

When routing is done, the receipt lists every page it touched, grouped by space. For each page you see:

- the change it made (a block added, replaced or removed, or a field changed);
- **Why here:** the engine's reason for choosing that page;
- whether the change is **Applied**, **Waiting for review** (the page is in a space with review on), or only a
  **Suggestion**.

Click a page's title to open it. If the receipt has an **Already known** section, those facts were already on your
pages, so nothing was changed for them.

Some facts may have no obvious home. They show under **Needs a home** at the top of the Capture page, where you can
pick a page for each one.

## Undo

- **Undo** on a page undoes every change the capture made to that page.
- **Undo all applied** undoes the whole capture at once.

If someone has edited a block since the capture changed it, Kinote leaves that block alone and marks it **Changed
since, not undone**, so you never lose a colleague's work.

## Wrong page?

If a change landed on the wrong page, click **Wrong page → move to…** under it, find the right page, and Kinote moves
the change there. If the capture became a new page in the wrong place, use **Wrong place → move to…** on the new page
instead.
