# Drawings

<ClipVideo name="drawings" label="A drawing is added to a page and sketched on" />

Sometimes a sketch says it better: a floor plan, a flow, a quick diagram. Drawings live right inside your pages, and
several people can draw on the same one at once.

## Add a drawing

1. On an empty line, type `/drawing` and choose **Drawing**.
2. Give it a title if you like.
3. Draw. Pick a tool from the toolbar, or use the keyboard: **R** for a rectangle, **O** for an ellipse, **A** for an
   arrow, **T** for text and **P** for a pen.

Drag the bottom edge to make the drawing taller, or click **Full screen** for more room.

## Working together

Everyone on the page sees each other's shapes as they're drawn, and everybody keeps what they drew.

Words you write in a drawing are searchable once the page is indexed. In a space with review on, a change to a
drawing waits for a reviewer like any other change, and **History** can bring back an earlier version of it.
