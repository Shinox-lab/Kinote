# Search and Ask

<ClipVideo name="ask" label="A question is answered with sources, and a source is opened" />

Search finds the passages in your pages and files that match what you're looking for, whether they use your exact
words or just mean the same thing. Ask goes one step further and writes an answer for you, citing where each part came
from.

You only ever see results from spaces you can read.

## Search

1. Click **Search** in the sidebar.
2. Type into **Search pages and files** and press Enter.
3. Narrow it down with **Scope** if you want to search one space.

Each result shows the passage and the page or file it came from. Click it to open the page at that spot. For a PDF, it
opens on the right page.

## Ask

1. Search for your question, for example *How many vacation days do new hires get?*
2. Click **Get an answer**.
3. Read the **Answer**. The **Sources** under it list the pages and files it used, with their version.
4. Click a source to open it.

Kinote only answers from what's in your wiki. If nothing there answers the question, it says
**No answer was found in the pages you can read.** rather than making something up.

Only indexed versions are used, so a change still waiting for review won't show up in answers until it's approved.
