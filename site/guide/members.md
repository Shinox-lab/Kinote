# Members and roles

Who can do what in Kinote depends on two things: your role in the **workspace**, and your role in each **space**.

## Workspace roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything an admin can. Only an owner can change another owner's role. |
| **Admin** | Manages members, groups and spaces, and can see and manage every space. |
| **Member** | Reads and edits the spaces that are shared with them. |
| **Viewer** | Reads only. |

## Inviting people

Admins and owners invite people from **Settings & members**. Choose **Admin**, **Member** or **Viewer** for each
invitation. The person gets a link to join the workspace.

## Space roles

Each space is shared with people or groups, each with a role. A role includes everything the roles above it can do.

| Role | What they can do in the space |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Reads pages and files. |
| **Editor** | Writes and edits pages, uploads files, and sends changes for review. |
| **Reviewer** | Approves or rejects changes, and can withdraw a page from search. |
| **Admin** | Manages the space's settings and who it's shared with, and archives pages. |

To share a space, open **Settings & members**, choose the space, and add people or groups with a role.

## AI agents

AI agents can be given access to a space too, but only as a **Viewer** or an **Editor**. They can never approve their
own changes or anyone else's. In a space with review on, their changes wait for a person.
