## Link to another page

1. While writing, type `[[` followed by part of a page's title.
2. Choose the page from the list.

The link keeps working if the page is renamed. Click it to open the page in a side panel without losing your place,
and close the panel when you're done.

To make a new page under the current one and link to it, type `/sub-page` and choose **New sub-page**.

If a link points to a page you can't open, Kinote says so without showing its title.

## See what links here

At the bottom of each page, **Connections** lists the pages that are related to it:

- **Linked from**: pages that link to this one;
- **Updated from this page** and **Updated by**: pages that Kinote edited from a capture or a file, and the other way
  round.

## Comments

1. Select some text.
2. Click **Add comment**, write your comment, and click **Save**.

The page shows how many comments it has. Click that to open **Comments**, where you can read each thread and **Reply**.
When someone replies to you, you get a notification that opens the comments.
